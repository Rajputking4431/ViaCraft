# Seller Guidelines

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to ViaCraft. These Seller Guidelines outline the standards, rules, and operational obligations required of all independent artisans ("Vendors" or "Sellers") registered on the ViaCraft Multi-Vendor Marketplace. Our platform is dedicated to maintaining high-quality, handcrafted resin products, customized resin art, and professional flower preservation services.

By registering as a Seller on ViaCraft, you agree to comply with these guidelines, the Vendor Terms & Conditions, and all other platform policies.

---

## 2. Vendor Registration & Verification
To sell on ViaCraft, Sellers must undergo onboarding and verification:
1.  Provide legal name, business type, phone number, physical workshop address, and active email.
2.  Provide valid tax and identification details (PAN card, GSTIN if applicable, and active bank account for settlements).
3.  Upload portfolio photos demonstrating craftsmanship.
4.  Maintain accurate store banners, logos, and descriptions via the Vendor Dashboard.

---

## 3. Marketplace Commission & Settlements
*   **Commission Structure:** ViaCraft charges a commission of **8% to 10%** (plus applicable taxes) on each completed order. This fee covers payment gateway integrations, hosting, traffic generation, and security.
*   **Weekly Disbursements:** Vendor settlements are batch-processed weekly on **every Wednesday** for all orders successfully delivered and completed in the previous cycle.
*   **Settlement Basis:** Settlements are calculated as: `Net Settlement = Product Price + Shipping Charges Collected - Platform Commission - Applicable Taxes/TDS`.

---

## 4. Product Listing & Quality Standards
*   **Handmade & Resin Specifics:** All listed items must be handcrafted, customized, or related to preservation art. Mass-produced commercial goods are strictly prohibited.
*   **No Placeholders or Stolen Images:** Sellers must upload high-quality, original images representing their actual craftsmanship. Using stolen photos from other creators or stock websites is prohibited.
*   **Descriptions & Variations:** Sellers must clearly state processing times, resin types used, dimensions, weight, and care instructions (e.g. keep away from direct sunlight/heat).

---

## 5. Packaging & Shipping Obligations
*   **Packaging Standards:** Resin products can be delicate and scratch-prone. Sellers must use bubble wrap, heavy-duty cardboard boxes, and protective corner guards to avoid transit damage.
*   **Logistics Channels:** Sellers must ship products through ViaCraft-integrated courier partners (e.g., Shiprocket) or update correct self-ship tracking details within **24 hours** of dispatch.
*   **Fulfillment Deadlines:** Sourced orders must be shipped within the declared processing window. Ready-to-ship orders must be dispatched within **48 hours**.

---

## 6. Prohibited & Restricted Items
Sellers are strictly barred from listing:
*   Non-handmade, factory-manufactured items.
*   Chemicals, raw uncured epoxy resins, or hazardous materials.
*   Counterfeit goods or items infringing on third-party intellectual property.
*   Offensive, obscene, or adult-themed resin crafts.

---

## 7. Policy Violations & Account Suspension
ViaCraft retains the right to issue warnings, freeze payouts, or suspend/terminate Vendor accounts if a Seller:
*   Fails to ship orders within the agreed timelines repeatedly.
*   Ships substandard, poorly cured, or damaged resin items.
*   Communicates abusively with customers.
*   Attempts to divert customers away from ViaCraft to complete payments externally.
*   Accumulates high return rates (greater than 5% of monthly orders).
