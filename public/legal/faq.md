# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. About ViaCraft
### 1.1. What is ViaCraft?
ViaCraft is a premium multi-vendor marketplace facilitator designed specifically for independent artisans specializing in handcrafted resin art, personalized gifts, custom resin jewellery, home decor, and luxury flower preservation.

### 1.2. Are products sold here mass-produced?
No. Every item listed on ViaCraft is designed, handcrafted, or customized by independent artisans. Due to the handmade nature of these products, minor variations in color, wood grain, flower placements, or micro-bubbles may occur. These are the unique marks of handcrafted items and are not defects.

---

## 2. Orders & Customizations
### 2.1. How do I place a custom or personalized order?
For products that support customization, you can specify text, initials, colors, or photos directly on the product detail page before adding the item to your cart. Once purchased, the Vendor will review your requirements and begin crafting.

### 2.2. What is the process for wedding bouquet or flower preservation?
For flower preservation orders:
1.  Place your order for the preservation keepsake block or jewellery on ViaCraft.
2.  Follow the instructions sent to your email to package and ship your fresh or dried flowers to the specific Vendor's workshop address at your own cost.
3.  Once the Vendor receives the flowers, they will professionally dry and cast them in premium, crystal-clear UV-resistant resin.
4.  The completed keepsake will be safely packaged and shipped back to you.

---

## 3. Shipping & Logistics
### 3.1. How long does shipping take?
Processing and delivery timelines vary depending on the product type:
*   **Ready-to-Ship Items:** Dispatched within 2 to 4 business days.
*   **Custom Handmade Products:** Take 7 to 14 business days to craft.
*   **Flower Preservation Keepsakes:** Take 4 to 8 weeks depending on the drying and curing process.
Please refer to the estimated timeline on the product page.

### 3.2. How can I track my order?
Once the Vendor dispatches your package, you will receive a tracking link via email. You can also monitor your shipment directly from the "Tracking" section in your Customer Dashboard.

---

## 4. Cancellation & Returns
### 4.1. Can I cancel my order?
*   **Standard Items:** Can be cancelled within 12 hours of booking, or until the Vendor accepts/dispatches the order, whichever is earlier.
*   **Custom/Preservation Orders:** Cannot be cancelled once the Vendor has accepted the order and started procurement or production.

### 4.2. What is your return policy?
Returns are accepted within **48 hours of delivery** only for items that are received damaged, incorrect, or feature manufacturing defects. Custom, personalized, or resin preservation items are strictly non-returnable. A continuous unboxing video is required for return approval.

---

## 5. Payments & Safety
### 5.1. Is it safe to pay on ViaCraft?
Yes. All payments on ViaCraft are processed securely via verified, RBI-compliant payment gateways (including Razorpay). We support UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular mobile wallets. ViaCraft does not store your card numbers or banking passwords.
