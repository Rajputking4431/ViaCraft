# Careers at ViaCraft

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. Work With Us
At ViaCraft, we are building India's premium multi-vendor marketplace for handcrafted resin art and custom memory preservation. We believe in the power of craftsmanship, technology, and design to help people turn fleeting moments into lifelong keepsakes.

We are a technology-driven, artisan-first team operating at the intersection of e-commerce, design, and technology.

---

## 2. Open Positions (Coming Soon)
We are currently scaling our engineering, operations, design, and artisan-relation teams. While our official jobs portal is under active development, we are always on the lookout for talented minds.

### 2.1. Areas of Growth
*   **Engineering & Tech:** Supabase, React, Vite, and cloud architecture specialists.
*   **Artisan Relations & Curation:** Managing onboarding, quality inspections, and training.
*   **Logistics & Operations:** Coordinating with logistics partners (e.g. Shiprocket) and managing custom order workflows.
*   **Content & Brand Design:** Photography, videography, and copywriters passionate about handmade crafts.
