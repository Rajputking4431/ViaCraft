# Return & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to ViaCraft ("we," "our," "us," or "Platform"). ViaCraft is a multi-vendor marketplace designed to support independent artisans specializing in handmade resin crafts, customized gifts, jewellery, and preservation art.

This Return & Refund Policy outlines the terms, guidelines, and procedures for requesting returns, refunds, replacements, or exchanges for products purchased on https://viacraft.in (the "Platform").

## 2. Platform Intermediary Role
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Marketplace Intermediary:** ViaCraft operates as a multi-vendor marketplace platform. Products are handcrafted, sold, and shipped by independent Vendors. All requests for returns, replacements, or refunds are submitted through the Platform and coordinated directly with the respective Vendor who fulfilled the order.

## 3. Return Eligibility Criteria

We strive to ensure complete customer satisfaction. However, due to the delicate, handcrafted, and customized nature of our products, returns are strictly restricted.

### 3.1. Eligible for Return
You may request a return or replacement only under the following conditions:
1.  **Damaged Product:** The product was received in a damaged condition due to shipping or handling.
2.  **Wrong Product:** The product delivered does not match the specifications of the ordered item (e.g., wrong size, design, or type).
3.  **Manufacturing Defect:** The product has an inherent structural defect that impairs its use.

*Note: All return claims must be initiated within **48 hours** of delivery. A continuous, unedited unboxing video starting from the sealed package is mandatory to substantiate claims of transit damage or incorrect items.*

### 3.2. Not Eligible for Return
The following categories of products are strictly **non-returnable** and **non-exchangeable**:
*   **Personalized Products:** Any item containing custom names, photographs, initials, dates, or specific custom text.
*   **Resin Preservation Orders:** Preservation blocks, keepsake jewellery, or custom castings made using the customer's physical flowers, ashes, hair, or other personal artifacts.
*   **Custom-Made Products:** Items manufactured or customized to specific user requests (e.g., custom colors, shapes, wood configurations).
*   **Used Products:** Any product that has been used, washed, altered, or is missing its original packaging, labels, tags, or protective padding.
*   **Minor Craft Variations:** Handcrafted resin products naturally feature minor variances in wood grain, flower placement, pigment shades, or tiny micro-bubbles. These are normal characteristics of artisanal craftsmanship and are not considered defects.

## 4. Replacement & Exchange Process

If you receive an eligible damaged, incorrect, or defective item, you may request a replacement or exchange:

1.  **Initiate Request:** Visit your Customer Dashboard or contact our support team at support@viacraft.in within 48 hours of delivery.
2.  **Submit Verification:** Provide your Order ID, clear photos of the defect, and the mandatory continuous unboxing video.
3.  **Vendor Review:** The Platform and the Vendor will review the submitted evidence within **48 hours**.
4.  **Reverse Shipping:** Once approved, we will arrange a reverse pickup from your registered shipping address at no extra cost. If reverse pickup is unavailable in your pin code, we will guide you to self-ship the item, and we will reimburse reasonable shipping costs.
5.  **Dispatch of Replacement:** The Vendor will craft and dispatch a replacement item once the original item is received back and inspected. Since resin pieces are handmade, crafting the replacement may take **7 to 14 business days**.

---

## 5. Refund Policy & Timelines

If a replacement is approved but the Vendor is unable to fulfill it due to stock constraints, material availability, or other factors, a refund will be processed.

### 5.1. Refund Approval
Refunds will only be initiated after:
- The returned product is received back at the Vendor’s facility.
- The Vendor verifies that the product is in its original, unused state (with tags/packaging intact).
- The Platform approves the refund request.

### 5.2. Refund Timelines
Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via our integrated payment gateway. The estimated timelines are:

| Payment Method | Estimated Processing Timeline |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Credit / Debit Cards** | 5 to 7 Business Days |
| **UPI / Net Banking** | 2 to 4 Business Days |
| **Wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, etc.)** | 24 to 48 Hours |

---

## 6. Order Cancellation Refunds
If you cancel an eligible order before it is dispatched, or if an order is cancelled by the Platform/Vendor due to stock unavailability, the refund will be automatically initiated to your original source of payment within **24 hours** and credited per the timelines above.
