# Help Center

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## 1. Welcome to ViaCraft Help Center
Our goal is to make your shopping experience as seamless and reliable as possible. If you need assistance with an order, payments, custom order specifications, flower preservation shipping instructions, or vendor registration, you are in the right place.

---

## 2. Dynamic Support Channels

Depending on your query type, please select the most efficient route:

### 2.1. Chat with a Seller (Order Queries)
If you have questions regarding the design, custom text, colors, shipping timeline, or packaging of an order, you can contact the independent Vendor directly:
1.  Log in to your Customer Account.
2.  Navigate to your Customer Dashboard.
3.  Click the "Chat" option next to your order details to send a direct message to the artisan.

### 2.2. Payment & Platform Disputes
For payment failures, double debits, platform fee queries, refund processing issues, or disputes with a Vendor:
*   **Email Support:** support@viacraft.in
*   **Response Timeline:** Within 24 to 48 hours.
*   **Hours of Operation:** Monday–Saturday, 10 AM – 7 PM

### 2.3. Seller Onboarding Support
For prospective artisans requiring assistance with store setups, tax documents (PAN/GSTIN), Cloudinary uploads, Supabase verification, or payout issues:
*   **Artisan Helpline Email:** support@viacraft.in
*   **Seller Dashboard Support:** Access the helpline widget inside your Vendor Dashboard.

## 4. Intermediary Escalation Process
If a Vendor fails to respond to your queries within **48 hours**, or if a dispute remains unresolved:
1.  Send an email to support@viacraft.in with the subject line `Dispute - Order #12345`.
2.  Provide a description of the issue, communication logs with the vendor, and any relevant media/proof.
3.  ViaCraft’s compliance team will review the dispute and issue a resolution (which may include a partial/full refund) within **5 business days**.
